## USAGE GUIDE

### Personalization Variables:
- [Name] - Recipient's first name
- [Company] - Company name
- [Job Title] - Recipient's role
- [Industry] - Their industry
- [Competitor] - Their competitor
- [Similar Company] - Case study company
- [Specific observation] - Research-based insight
- [Pain point] - Known challenge
- [Benefit] - Your value proposition
- [Metric] - Quantifiable result

### Best Practices:
1. Always personalize the first line
2. Keep emails under 150 words
3. Include ONE clear call-to-action
4. Use specific numbers and results
5. Reference their company/industry
6. Follow up 3-4 times
7. A/B test subject lines
8. Send Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm

## RESPONSE RATE OPTIMIZATION

### High-Performing Elements:
✅ Personalized first line
✅ Specific numbers/metrics
✅ Social proof (case studies)
✅ Clear value proposition
✅ Single CTA
✅ Brief (under 150 words)
✅ Mobile-friendly formatting

### Avoid:
❌ Generic templates
❌ Multiple CTAs
❌ Long paragraphs
❌ Salesy language
❌ Attachments in first email
❌ Asking for too much time
